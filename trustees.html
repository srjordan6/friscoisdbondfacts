1. The request, October 20, 2026

Sent individually to all seven trustees, each addressed by name. Subject: "Request: end new purchases from Lone Star Furnishings."

Trustee [name], I'm writing as a Frisco ISD parent and taxpayer to ask the Board to direct the administration to stop placing new orders with Lone Star Furnishings, LLC. Both required conflict disclosures are now public: the vendor's Form CIQ, filed October 15, and Superintendent Fouche's Form CIS, signed April 30 and posted October 20. They confirm that the superintendent's spouse is the vendor's VP of Sales and receives income from it. The District has paid this vendor $50.4 million since fiscal year 2014, most of it from bond funds, including $2.5 million in fiscal year 2026. I take the District at its word that Mrs. Fouche is walled off from Frisco ISD business and that Dr. Fouche stays out of these purchases. But disclosure is the floor, not the fix. The District should not keep sending money to a company that employs the superintendent's spouse as a sales executive, however good the paperwork is. This vendor doesn't make the furniture. Smith System, one of the main lines it sells, holds a statewide BuyBoard contract that lets Texas districts order directly from the manufacturer. The District can buy the same furniture without this vendor. My specific request is that the Board direct the administration to place no new purchase orders with Lone Star Furnishings while this relationship exists, honor existing orders and warranties, and report publicly when that's in place. With a bond proposal coming, voters will want to know where each trustee stands on this, and I'll be making it part of the public conversation. I'd welcome your response. The documents are here: friscoisdbondfacts.com/#disclosure Stephen Jordan Frisco ISD parent

3. Reply to Trustee Wamhoff, November 13, 2026

Trustee Wamhoff, Thank you for responding. I appreciate it. This is not a personnel matter. The District buying furniture from a vendor that employs the superintendent's spouse, with conflict disclosures on file from both the vendor and the superintendent, is not private. Those disclosures are public records, and so are the District's payments to this vendor. My request is about a purchasing practice: whether the District should keep placing new orders with Lone Star Furnishings while this relationship exists, when the same product lines can be bought directly from the manufacturer through BuyBoard. Would you be willing to ask that the Board consider this as a purchasing practice, separate from any personnel discussion? Stephen Jordan
