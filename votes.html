How each trustee votes

Updated November 25, 2026

Every vote in the Board's signed minutes, by trustee, from October 20, 2025 through October 27, 2026: 121 votes at 24 meetings. Earlier signed minutes are not posted on the District's meeting site. The most important vote ahead is whether to call a bond election, expected in early 2027. Split votes are highlighted. A dash means the minutes do not list that trustee for the vote; a blank means the trustee was not on the Board yet, or had left. Download the data (CSV)

Summary

112 of 121 votes were unanimous. No trustee voted against a budget, a tax rate, a contract or a purchase. The only no votes were on library book lists, a missed school day waiver, and individual student grievance and expulsion appeals.
