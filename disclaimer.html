1. Purpose

friscoisdbondfacts.com presents Frisco ISD's own financial records, bond filings, and state data so voters can review them before the 2027 bond election. 2. Sources and dates

Figures come from public records, including Frisco ISD budgets, board documents, check registers, and financial reports; bond filings on the MSRB's EMMA system; the Texas Education Agency; the Texas Comptroller; and the Texas Ethics Commission. Each figure is current as of the document or date cited. Records change, and the District may publish newer numbers after a page is written.

3. Illustrations are not projections

Some features show what could happen under stated assumptions, including the debt tax calculator and the dashed "new debt" outline on the debt chart. These are illustrations, not forecasts, and not Frisco ISD projections. Their assumptions are stated next to them.
