Board meetings

Each Frisco ISD board meeting gets its own page here: a short, plain-English summary of what is worth knowing, followed by every public document from the agenda, saved so it stays available. We post a preview once the agenda is published and add the results after the meeting.

Texas law requires the Board to post its agenda at least 72 hours before a regular meeting, so there is a short window to read the documents before the vote. Most purchasing and vendor items sit on the consent agenda, which the Board usually approves in one vote without discussion. That is why we read the attachments, not just the item titles.

On each meeting page we look for the items that affect taxpayers most: quarterly budget reports and the size of the general fund reserve, budget amendments, purchases over $100,000, new purchasing cooperatives and vendor approvals that renew for years, and anything tied to bonds or debt. Coverage of the November 16, 2026 meeting is on the home page, and each trustee's recorded votes are on the votes page.
